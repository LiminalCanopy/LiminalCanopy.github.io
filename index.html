Liminal Canopy

The canopy doesn’t shelter the in-between.
The in-between brings the canopy into operation.

Liminal Canopy is form-in-operation: a threshold effect where sound, text, residue, visual studies, and evidence gather into one time-feel.

Atmosphere over architecture.
Refined form under non-sterile contact.
Beauty, brutality, residue, and evidence under one shifting canopy.
